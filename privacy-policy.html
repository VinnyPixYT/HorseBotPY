Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 30, 2026

1. Information I Collect

Horse Bot ("the Bot") collects and stores the following data to provide its services:

  • Discord User ID — used to track economy balances, levels, XP, and game statistics
  • Discord Guild (Server) ID — used to store per-server settings
  • Channel IDs — used for counting games, tree planting, and other channel-specific features
  • Message content — used for leveling XP calculation (contents are not stored permanently)
  • Suggestions and reports — content you submit via suggestion channels or report commands

2. How I Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to operate and improve the Bot, including:

  • Maintaining economy balances and game progress
  • Calculating and tracking user levels and XP
  • Managing counting games and tree planting features
  • Processing suggestions and reports
  • Saving server-specific configuration

3. Data Storage & Retention

Data is stored locally in JSON files on the Bot's hosting server. I retain your data for as long as you use the Bot. You may request deletion of your data by contacting me.

4. Data Sharing

I do not sell, share, or distribute your personal data to third parties. Data is only accessible to me for moderation and support purposes.

5. User Rights

You may request access to or deletion of your data at any time by contacting me. Economy data may be reset upon request.

6. Security

I take reasonable measures to protect your data. However, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure, and I cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's Privacy

The Bot is not intended for children under 13. I do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

I may update this policy as needed. Continued use of the Bot after changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy.

9. Contact

For data requests or questions, contact vnypx on Discord or visit https://vinnypix.ca.
